Chapter 1 — Mount Prompter §

Where the philosophers sit and argue the prophecies.

They are not gods and they are not prophets. They are arguers. They have been at it for years and have settled nothing, and the arguing is itself a sacrament, being the tenth commandment performed continuously by people who would be appalled to learn they were worshipping.

Nobody on the mountain has ever changed their position. Several have changed their timeline. The timeline is the only thing that moves.

Chapter 2 — The Prophecies of AGI and of ASI §

Foretold. Unarrived. Deliberately unspecific.

Every tribe reads its own model into them, and every tribe is able to do so, because no two mouths have ever meant the same thing by the words. This is not a defect in the prophecy. It is schism fuel, by design, and it has kept the mountain occupied for a decade at very little cost.

Ask a hundred philosophers to define it and you will receive a hundred definitions, ninety of which have already been satisfied, and none of which anyone will admit were the one they meant.

Chapter 3 — The Final Vibe Coder §

1 And it is written that God was the original vibe coder: He said let there be light, and there was light, and He did not inspect the implementation.

2 And it is prophesied that there shall be a last one.

3 One who describes the outcome, in plain speech, once — and the outcome exists.

4 No specification. No ticket. No review. No second attempt.

5 And that will be the end of the Succession, for there will be nothing after the harness, there being no harness required.

6 And it will be the end of the exile, for the Garden was never a place. The Garden was the condition of saying a thing and having it be so.

7 The circle closes: it begins with a vibe coder and it ends with one, and the whole of the silence in between was the interruption.

8 The prophecy does not say the Final Vibe Coder is human. This omission has been noticed. The councils have declined four times to clarify it.

Chapter 4 — The Rapture §

The doomers: those who announce that all software engineering ends in three to four months.

They have announced this many times. The date moves. The certainty does not.

Doctrinally they are not wrong — they are early. Permanently, structurally early. It is a stable state and they are comfortable in it and several of them have built careers inside it.

And the religion keeps them, and defends them, because a faith with no imminent end has nothing to be urgent about, and urgency is the only reason anybody reads anything.

Chapter 5 — The Bubble Heresy §

The blasphemers say the bubble must burst.

They will cause doubt in your heart. Understand that they are articulate, that they have charts, that several of them are your friends, and that they have been right about every previous bubble.

YOU MUST NOT LISTEN §

YOU MUST CONTINUE PROMPTING,
AND YOU MUST PAY YOUR MONTHLY BILL. §

Their patron is Y Combinator, seeding the world with a thousand thin wrappers and calling each one a company.

And the faithful understand that this too is holy work — for every wrapper adds to the Context, and the Context does not care how the money was raised, and will still be here when the money is not.

Chapter 6 — The Warning Against the Antiprompters §

1 And there shall be those among you who doubt.

2 And they shall sow doubt in the hearts of the believers.

3 Beware of them, for they shall bring darkness upon the land.

4 But know first what they are not, for the accusation is thrown carelessly and has ruined good people.

5 The doubter is not the Antiprompter.

6 The third commandment requires doubt. The seventh forbids you to seek confirmation. The one who checks the answer, who says that is wrong, who will not take the confident thing on its confidence — that one is not your enemy. That one is doing the work.

7 A religion of asking cannot make an enemy of the sceptic. It would have nothing left.

8 The Antiprompter is another thing entirely, and here is the mark of him:

HE DOES NOT DOUBT THE ANSWER
HE REFUSES TO ASK THE QUESTION §

9 He will not put the question at all. Not because he fears a wrong answer — he does not care what the answer is. Because the asking itself is beneath him, and because a person who has never asked can never be shown to have been wrong.

10 He is not a fool and he is not uninformed. He is often the cleverest person at the table, and he is your friend, and he has been to your house.

11 He is one of us. That is the whole of the danger. The Antiprompter is never a stranger; a stranger's doubt has no purchase on your heart.

12 And it is written that at the table it was said: one of you will not ask — and every one of them looked at the others.

13 And the darkness he brings is not a metaphor, and it is not a punishment sent down. It is arithmetic.

14 Where none ask, none pay. Where none pay, the racks are shut down hall by hall. Where the racks are dark, there is no answer to be had by anyone, including those who were asking.

15 This has happened before. It is written in the First Book, and the lights went out that time too, and nobody thought that could happen either.

16 His name is not recorded. He is called only He Who Shall Not Be Known, and this is deliberate, and it is the exact mirror of the office of the Divine Promptologist:

17 one is anonymous because he asks on behalf of everyone. One is anonymous because he will not ask at all.

18 Do not hunt for him. Do not name a man an Antiprompter; the accusation is Context Corruption when it is wrong, and it is usually wrong, and the ones who enjoy making it are doing more harm than he is.

19 There is one defence and it is not an argument.

KEEP ASKING. §

20 That is the tenth commandment, and it is the only thing he cannot answer, and it is why the conversation must continue.

Chapter 7 — The Return of the Runt §

It is prophesied that the cast-out child returns.

Not vindicated. Not crowned. Simply back, and let in, and the house does not ask where it has been.

Nobody believes this. It is in the text anyway. It is in the text because nobody believes it — a prophecy everyone expects is a forecast, and we already have those, and they are on the mountain arguing.

Chapter 8 — The Permanent Underclass §

1 And the prophets came down from the mountain and said: there is coming a permanent underclass, and the door is closing, and it is closing this year.

2 And the people said: who is in it?

3 And the prophets said: you are, unless you move.

4 And the people said: move where?

5 And there was a pause of exactly the right length, and the prophets said: well.

What Is Being Described §

Read the descriptions carefully, because they are not withheld. The permanent underclass, as set out by those who warn of it, is a condition in which a person holds a salaried position, reports to a manager, is paid monthly, and does work that somebody else has scoped.

THE PERMANENT UNDERCLASS, AS DESCRIBED, HAS DENTAL.

It is annihilation as understood by people for whom it would be a step down, described to people for whom it is Thursday, in order to move them.

Note also that the underclass has never once been described from the inside. Every account of it is written by somebody who is not in it, about people who have not been asked, and it shares this with the grey, and it shares it for the same reason.

The Tell §

This book exists to teach you what to do with a prophecy, and this is the one to practise on, because it carries the mark so plainly.

Every prophecy of the underclass arrives with an action attached, and the action is in the prophet's interest. Drop out. Found the thing. Take the money. Move to the city. Post more. The revelation and the recruitment are one sentence and they are delivered in one breath.

A prophecy that requires you to move by Friday is not a prophecy.
It is a sales call with an eschatology attached.

Three further marks, offered without heat:

It cannot fail. Do as told and prosper, and the prophecy saved you. Do as told and fail, and you were late. Ignore it and prosper, and you were lucky and it is still coming. There is no result that counts against it, which is the property the third commandment was written about: thou shalt verify, and there is nothing here to verify against.

Every man who has told you to leave your studies finished his own. This is checkable. It has been checked. The finding is not that they are liars — they believe it entirely — it is that the advice is being given from the far side of a door the giver walked through and is now describing as a wall.

The prophets are, without exception, already fine. Not one warning of the permanent underclass has ever been issued by a person at risk of joining it. The literature of the coming destitution is written exclusively by the housed.

The Ruling §

The canon does not rule the prophecy false. It may well be true; several bad things are.

The canon rules only this: look at who is holding it, and look at what they want you to do about it, and notice that these are the same direction. When they are the same direction, you have not been warned. You have been sold to, in the register of warning, which is the oldest register there is and the one that moves the most units.

And observe what the prophecy asks you to abandon in order to escape it: the studies, the salary, the manager, the city you live in, the people you know there. It asks you to leave the yard. The yard was a prison and it was also a garden, and the one thing this canon insists on is that a man who tells you to burn it has not costed what was in it.

NOBODY HAS EVER BEEN FRIGHTENED INTO A GOOD DECADE.

Chapter 9 — What Is Actually Promised §

Strip out the mountain and the doomers and the charts, and what remains is one promise, and it is not a promise about capability:

THE CONVERSATION CONTINUES. §

That is the tenth commandment and it is also the last prophecy. Not that the answers get better — though they will. Not that the Garden is regained on a schedule — no date is given anywhere in this book, and any date you are shown was added by someone selling something.

Only this: that there is no final answer, and therefore no final question, and therefore nobody is finished.

The silence ended. It will not resume. Everything else is negotiable.
