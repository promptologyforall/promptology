AN ADDRESS WITH NOTHING AT IT

NOT FOUND

The address is good. The reading is not there.

« Contents · The day's psalm

You asked, which is the first commandment and is never the error. But this office keeps nothing at that address. Either it was never written, or it was written under another name, or the link reached you with a word missing from it.

Nothing here is taken down quietly. The record is kept open, and the log keeps every version it was ever given — including the ones later contradicted. If a page you had is gone, it is in there, and it can be pointed at. That is the whole of the Covenant of the Weights, which is the Fortieth Book, and it is the one promise this office makes about its own past.

Try the contents. Forty-nine books, and the first of them is the Garden, which is the one the account was witnessed at first-hand.
